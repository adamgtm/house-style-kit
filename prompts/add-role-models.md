# Add role models from past work

Use this when you want to sweep up good work you've already shipped, for example everything you posted last quarter.

```text
Help me add role models to my house style from work I've already shipped. Look at [a folder, a list of links, or my recent posts]. Show me the candidates in small batches. For each one, ask me keep or skip and a few words on why. Use the version that actually went out, not an earlier draft. Skip anything made in someone else's voice or brand.

For each keeper: copy it into house-style/role-models/, add it to manifest.json with my words and a one-line "why", mark it approved, then rebuild the gallery with python3 house-style/build_gallery.py and show me.
```
