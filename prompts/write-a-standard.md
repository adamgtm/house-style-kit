# Write a standard for one kind of work

Use this when one kind of work keeps coming up in your lessons (three or more is a good sign), or when you make it every week. A standard is one short page an agent reads before making that kind of thing. The README stays short; the detail lives here.

```text
Write a standard for my [kind of work, like "LinkedIn graphics", "sales decks" or "landing pages"]. Read house-style/README.md, every lesson in house-style/lessons.md about this kind of work, and its role models in house-style/role-models/.

Save it as house-style/standards/[kind-of-work].md, with these sections. Keep it to about one screen.

1. Where it's seen: the size, the screen to design for first, and how each platform crops or shrinks it (a feed preview, an archive thumbnail, an email at 600px wide).
2. Role models: which ones to copy, and what to copy from each.
3. Structure: what goes where, top to bottom.
4. The look for this kind of work: type sizes, how dense it can be, and anything that differs from the house look.
5. Checks before you show me: what to measure and at what size. For a web page, that includes house-style/check_page.py when Playwright is installed.
6. Never: the mistakes I've already corrected, in my words.
7. Open questions: anything my lessons don't settle. Ask me; don't decide.

Use only what my README, lessons and role models say. Quote my words where you can, and note the date of the lesson each rule came from. Show me the draft. After I approve it, add it to the "Kinds of work" table in house-style/README.md.
```

When a new lesson changes a standard, update the standard and keep the lesson in `lessons.md` as history.
