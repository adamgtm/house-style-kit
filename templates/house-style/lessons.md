# House style lessons

Lessons from my edits, newest first. Each one answers four things:

- **The rule**, in one sentence.
- **The source:** the date and what we were making.
- **My exact words**, or the before and after.
- **The kind of work** it applies to (graphics, charts, slides, pages, email, or everything).

When a lesson keeps coming back, it becomes a rule in `README.md`. When a newer lesson disagrees with an older one, the newer one wins: update the rule and keep both entries here as history.
